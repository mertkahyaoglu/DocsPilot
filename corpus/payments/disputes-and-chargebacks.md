# Disputes and Chargebacks

A dispute, also called a chargeback, happens when a cardholder asks their bank to reverse a payment you received.

## What happens when a dispute is opened

- The disputed amount is removed from your balance straight away.
- A $15 dispute fee is charged. The fee is refunded if you win.
- You receive an email and the dispute appears at Payments → Disputes.

## Responding

You have 7 days from the notification to submit evidence. If you miss the deadline, the dispute is lost automatically.

To respond:

1. Go to Payments → Disputes and open the dispute.
2. Select Submit evidence.
3. Upload supporting files and write a short explanation.
4. Select Submit. You cannot edit your response after submitting.

Evidence files can be PDF, JPG, or PNG, up to 5 MB each, and you can attach up to 10 files.

## Useful evidence

- The sent invoice and the date the customer viewed it
- Proof of delivery or of the service being used
- Email conversations with the customer
- Your terms and the customer's acceptance

## Outcome

The card network usually decides within 60 days of your submission. If you win, the amount and the fee are returned to your balance. If you lose, they are not returned.

## Accepting a dispute

If you agree with the cardholder, choose Accept dispute. The dispute is closed immediately, and the $15 fee still applies.

## Reducing disputes

Use a clear business name in your Business profile so it matches what appears on card statements, send invoices promptly, and issue refunds for obvious mistakes before the customer contacts their bank.
