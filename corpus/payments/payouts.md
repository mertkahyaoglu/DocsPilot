# Payouts to Your Bank Account

A payout is a transfer from your Acme Billing balance to your bank account. Payouts are automatic.

## Setting up

Go to Settings → Payouts → Bank account and enter your bank details. Until a bank account is added and verified, payments accumulate in your balance and nothing is paid out.

## Timing

After a payment succeeds, the net amount is included in a payout:

- **Starter and Growth:** 2 business days after the payment succeeds.
- **Scale:** 1 business day after the payment succeeds.

ACH and SEPA payments start counting from the day they settle, not the day the customer submitted them.

Payouts are not sent on weekends or bank holidays.

## Minimum payout

The minimum payout is $10. If your balance is under that amount, it is carried over to the next payout and nothing is sent.

## What is deducted

The amount of each payout equals your payments minus processing fees, refunds, dispute amounts, and dispute fees. Plan fees are billed separately and are not taken from payouts.

## Payout statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| pending | Scheduled but not yet sent to the bank. |
| in_transit | Sent and on its way to your bank. |
| paid | Arrived in your bank account. |
| failed | The bank rejected the transfer. |

## Changing your bank account

When you change your bank account, the next payout is held for 72 hours as a security measure. You will get an email at the old and the new contact addresses. Only the Owner or an Admin can change bank details.

## Failed payouts

A payout fails if the account is closed or the details are wrong. The funds return to your balance, and you receive an email. Correct the bank details and the next scheduled payout will include the returned amount.

## Reconciliation

Go to Payments → Payouts and open a payout to see every payment it contains. You can export the list as CSV for your bookkeeping.
