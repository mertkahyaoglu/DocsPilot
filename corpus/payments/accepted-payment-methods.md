# Accepted Payment Methods

Your customers can pay invoices on the hosted invoice page using the methods below. Fees for each method are listed on the Pricing and Plans page.

## Cards

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover credit and debit cards are accepted. Card payments are confirmed in seconds. When the customer's bank asks for extra verification, 3-D Secure is started automatically and the payment completes once the customer approves it.

## ACH bank debit

ACH is available for US bank accounts and USD invoices only. The customer enters their routing and account number and confirms with two small verification deposits or instant bank login. ACH payments take up to 4 business days to settle, and the invoice stays in "Processing" until then.

## SEPA direct debit

SEPA is available for EUR invoices to customers with a bank account in the SEPA area. Settlement takes up to 5 business days.

## Saved payment methods

When a customer pays, they can choose Save for future payments. Saved methods are used for automatic collection on recurring invoices and subscriptions. Customers can remove a saved method at any time from the link in any receipt email.

## Payment links

Go to Payments → Payment links to create a shareable link for a fixed amount. The link opens a hosted checkout page. You can set an expiry date of up to 90 days.

## Partial payments

On Growth and Scale, you can allow partial payments on an invoice at Settings → Invoices → Defaults → Allow partial payments. The invoice shows the remaining balance until it is fully paid.

## Currencies

A customer always pays in the currency of the invoice. See the Currency Settings page for how to set invoice currencies.

## Receipts

A receipt email is sent automatically after each successful payment. You can turn this off at Settings → Payments → Receipts.
