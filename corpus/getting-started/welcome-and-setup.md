# Welcome and Account Setup

Acme Billing is invoicing and subscription billing software for small businesses. This guide takes you from sign-up to your first payout.

## Creating your account

You can sign up with an email address and password, or with Google login. Both methods create the same kind of account. After sign-up we send a verification email, and the link in it is valid for 24 hours. If the link expires, choose "Resend verification email" on the sign-in page.

## Free trial

Every new account starts with a 14-day free trial of Growth plan features. The trial lasts 14 days and no credit card is required to start it. When the trial ends, the account becomes read-only until you choose a plan under Settings → Billing → Plan.

## Setup checklist

Work through these steps before you send your first invoice:

1. **Business profile:** add your legal name, address, and Tax ID at Settings → Business profile.
2. **Currency:** pick your default currency at Settings → Billing → Currency.
3. **Branding:** upload a logo and choose an accent colour at Settings → Invoices → Branding.
4. **Payouts:** connect the bank account that receives your money at Settings → Payouts → Bank account.
5. **Team:** invite colleagues at Settings → Team → Invite member.

## Getting help

In the app, choose Help → Contact support to open a support ticket. You can also email support@acmebilling.example. Support is staffed Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 UTC.

Target first-response times depend on your plan:

- Starter: 1 business day
- Growth: 4 business hours
- Scale: 1 hour (priority queue)

Acme Billing support will never ask you to send your password or API key by email or chat.
