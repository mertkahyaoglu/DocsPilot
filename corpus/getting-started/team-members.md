# Team Members and Roles

You can invite colleagues, your bookkeeper, or your accountant to your Acme Billing workspace and control what each person can do.

## Inviting someone

1. Go to Settings → Team → Invite member.
2. Enter their email address and choose a role.
3. Select Send invite.

Invitations expire after 7 days. You can resend an expired invitation from the same page.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including billing, plan changes, and deleting the account. Each account has exactly one Owner. |
| Admin | Manage invoices, customers, payments, settings, and team members. Cannot change the plan or delete the account. |
| Accountant | Create and edit invoices, issue credit notes, view payments and reports. Cannot change settings. |
| Viewer | Read-only access to invoices, customers, and reports. |

## Seats

Every person in your workspace uses one seat, whatever their role. The number of seats included in your plan is listed on the Pricing and Plans page. Pending invitations do not use a seat until they are accepted.

If you need more seats than your plan includes, extra seats are added automatically and billed as described in the Overage Charges page. You do not need to contact support.

## Transferring ownership

Only the current Owner can transfer ownership. Go to Settings → Team, open the member's menu, and choose Make owner. You will become an Admin. Transfers take effect immediately and the new Owner receives a confirmation email.

## Removing a member

Open Settings → Team, choose the member's menu, then Remove. Their seat is freed straight away. Invoices and notes they created stay in the account and are shown with their name.
