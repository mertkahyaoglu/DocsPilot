# Overage Charges

Acme Billing never stops you from sending an invoice or adding a teammate because you reached a plan limit. Instead, usage above your plan's included amount is billed as overage.

## Invoice overage

Each invoice sent beyond your plan's monthly allowance is charged at a per-invoice rate:

| Plan | Overage rate per invoice |
|---|---|
| Starter | $0.40 |
| Growth | $0.25 |
| Scale | $0.15 |

For example, a Starter account that sends 65 invoices in a billing cycle has 15 invoices over the allowance. The overage is 15 × $0.40 = $6.00.

## Extra seats

Team seats beyond the number included in your plan cost:

| Plan | Price per extra seat |
|---|---|
| Starter | $5 per month |
| Growth | $4 per month |
| Scale | $3 per month |

Extra seats are prorated by the day in the month you add them.

## When you are billed

Overage and extra seat charges are added to your next plan invoice. They are not charged separately in the middle of a cycle.

## Usage alerts

The Owner and all Admins receive an email when you reach 80% of your invoice allowance and again at 100%. You can see current usage at Settings → Billing → Usage.

## When the counter resets

Invoice usage resets at the start of each billing cycle, which begins on the day you subscribed or last changed plan. It is not tied to the calendar month.

## Limits that are never exceeded

Active subscriptions are a hard limit and do not have an overage rate. If you reach the limit for your plan, you must cancel an existing subscription or upgrade before you can create a new one. See the Changing Plans page for how upgrades work.
