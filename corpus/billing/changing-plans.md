# Changing Plans

You can move between Starter, Growth, and Scale at any time. Only the account Owner can change the plan.

## How to change

Go to Settings → Billing → Plan, choose the new plan, and confirm.

## Upgrades

Upgrades take effect immediately. You are charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current billing cycle, and the higher limits for invoices, seats, and API rate are available straight away. Your next full charge is at the new plan price.

## Downgrades

Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle, not immediately. Until then you keep all the features and limits of your current plan. You will not receive a credit for the remaining days.

A downgrade is blocked if your account is above the limits of the target plan. Before you can downgrade you must:

- Remove team members until you are within the seat count of the lower plan.
- Cancel active subscriptions until you are within the lower plan's subscription limit.

Features that are not part of the lower plan, such as recurring invoices, stop working at the downgrade. Existing recurring schedules are paused, not deleted.

## Switching between monthly and annual billing

- **Monthly to annual:** takes effect immediately. Unused monthly time is credited toward the annual price.
- **Annual to monthly:** takes effect at your next renewal date.

## Billing date

Changing plans does not change the day of the month you are billed unless you upgrade, in which case the billing day resets to the day of the upgrade.

## Trial accounts

If you are still within the 14-day free trial, choosing a plan ends the trial immediately and starts your first billing cycle.
