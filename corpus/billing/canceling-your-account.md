# Canceling Your Account

You can cancel your Acme Billing plan whenever you like. Only the account Owner can cancel.

## How to cancel

Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel plan and follow the prompts. You will be asked to confirm by entering your password, or by re-authenticating with Google if you signed up with Google login.

## What happens at cancellation

- You keep full access until the end of the period you have already paid for.
- No further charges are made after that date.
- Cancellation does not by itself trigger a refund. Refund requests are covered by the refund policy.
- Any active subscriptions you were billing your own customers through Acme Billing are cancelled when your plan ends, and no more charges are attempted.

## The 90-day read-only period

After your paid period ends, your workspace becomes read-only for 90 days. During this period you can:

- Log in and view invoices, customers, and payments.
- Export your data at Settings → Data → Export, in CSV or JSON format.
- Reactivate your plan, which restores full access with all data intact.

You cannot create or send invoices, or take payments, while the account is read-only.

## After 90 days

Once the 90 days have passed, customer personal data is permanently deleted. Financial records such as invoice amounts, dates, and tax details are kept in anonymised form as described in the Data Retention page.

## Deleting immediately

If you want your personal data removed before the 90 days are over, go to Settings → Data → Request deletion. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
