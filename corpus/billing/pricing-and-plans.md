# Pricing and Plans

Acme Billing has three paid plans: Starter, Growth, and Scale. All prices are in US dollars and exclude any applicable taxes.

## Plan comparison

| | Starter | Growth | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly price | $19 per month | $59 per month | $199 per month |
| Annual price | $190 per year | $590 per year | $1,990 per year |
| Invoices included per month | 50 | 500 | 5,000 |
| Team seats included | 2 | 10 | 50 |
| Active subscriptions | 10 | 250 | Unlimited |
| API rate limit | 60 requests per minute | 300 requests per minute | 1,200 requests per minute |
| Recurring invoices | No | Yes | Yes |
| Multi-currency invoicing | No | Yes | Yes |
| Automatic late fees | No | Yes | Yes |
| Support first response | 1 business day | 4 business hours | 1 hour |

## Annual billing

Paying annually gives you two months free. The annual price is ten times the monthly price.

## Free trial

New accounts get a 14-day free trial with Growth plan features. No credit card is required.

## Payment processing fees

Processing fees are separate from your plan price. They are deducted from each payment before it is paid out to you.

| Payment method | Fee |
|---|---|
| Card | 2.9% + $0.30 per successful charge |
| International card | Additional 1.5% |
| ACH bank debit | 0.8%, capped at $5 per payment |
| SEPA direct debit | 0.8%, capped at €5 per payment |

## What counts toward your invoice limit

Every invoice that leaves the Draft status counts toward the monthly limit, including invoices generated by recurring schedules. Voided invoices still count. Draft invoices do not count until they are sent.

If you go over the included number of invoices, you are not blocked. Extra invoices are billed as overage; see the Overage Charges page.
