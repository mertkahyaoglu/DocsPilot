# Subscription Refund Policy

This policy describes when you can get your money back for Acme Billing subscription charges.

## Eligibility

You can request a refund within 14 days of the charge date. The policy applies to the plan charges of the Starter, Growth, and Scale plans, whether they are billed monthly or annually.

## What is refunded

- The full amount of the plan charge is refunded.
- Overage and extra seat charges are refunded only when they were charged in error.
- Payment processing fees on your customers' payments are not affected by this policy.

## How to request a refund

Go to Settings → Billing → Request refund and select the charge. You can also email support@acmebilling.example from the address of the account Owner. Only the Owner can request refunds.

## Timing

Requests are reviewed within 5 business days. Once approved, the money appears on your statement in 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

## After the window

Charges older than 14 days are not refundable. You can still cancel your plan to avoid future charges; see Canceling Your Account.
