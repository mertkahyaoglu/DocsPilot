# Acceptable Use Policy

Acme Billing is built for legitimate small businesses. This policy explains what you may not do with the service.

## Prohibited use

You may not use Acme Billing to:

- Bill for goods or services that are illegal in your country or in your customer's country.
- Send invoices that misrepresent who you are or what you are selling.
- Collect payments for businesses other than your own without our written agreement.
- Attempt to bypass rate limits, plan limits, or security controls.
- Upload malware, or probe the service for vulnerabilities without permission.

## Restricted businesses

Some businesses carry high dispute risk and need approval before you can take card payments. These include travel booking, ticket resale, and pre-order sales that deliver more than 60 days after payment. Contact support before you start billing for these.

## Enforcement

If we think you have broken this policy we will:

1. Email the account Owner with a description of the issue.
2. Give you 7 days to respond or fix the problem.
3. Suspend the account if the problem continues or if there is a risk to others.

In severe cases, such as suspected fraud, we may suspend the account immediately and tell you why by email.

## Suspended accounts

A suspended account is read-only. You can still log in and export your data, but you cannot send invoices or take payments. Payouts of your existing balance continue unless a legal hold applies.

## Appeals

Reply to the suspension email within 30 days to appeal. A different member of the support team reviews every appeal and replies within 5 business days.

## Changes to this policy

We will email the Owner at least 30 days before any change takes effect.
