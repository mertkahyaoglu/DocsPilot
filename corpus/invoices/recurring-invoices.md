# Recurring Invoices and Subscriptions

Recurring invoices let you bill the same customer on a schedule without creating each invoice by hand. They are available on the Growth and Scale plans only. Starter accounts can duplicate invoices manually but cannot create schedules.

## Creating a recurring schedule

1. Go to Invoices → Recurring → New schedule.
2. Choose the customer and add line items as you would for a normal invoice.
3. Choose a frequency: weekly, monthly, quarterly, or yearly.
4. Set a start date and, optionally, an end date or a number of cycles.
5. Choose whether invoices are sent automatically or saved as drafts for your review.

Invoices are generated at 06:00 UTC on each scheduled date.

## Automatic collection

If the customer has a saved card or bank account, you can turn on Collect automatically. Acme Billing will then charge the payment method on the invoice's due date. If the charge fails, the retry rules described in the Failed Payments and Dunning page apply.

## Subscriptions

A subscription ties a customer to a recurring price and always collects automatically. Subscriptions are managed through Customers → open customer → Subscriptions or through `POST /v2/subscriptions`. The number of active subscriptions you can have depends on your plan: 10 on Starter, 250 on Growth, and unlimited on Scale.

## Pausing and ending

- **Pause:** open the schedule and choose Pause. No invoices are generated until you resume. Missed dates are not backfilled.
- **End:** choose End schedule. Invoices already created are not affected.

## Invoice limits

Every invoice generated by a schedule counts toward your plan's monthly invoice allowance. A schedule that bills 20 customers monthly uses 20 invoices each cycle.

## Editing a schedule

Changes apply to future invoices only. To change an invoice that has already been generated, edit that invoice directly.

## If you downgrade

When you move to a plan without recurring invoices, all schedules are paused and kept. They resume if you upgrade again.
